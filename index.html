Open the Foul-Mouthed Oracle

If it has been asleep it shows "This app has gone to sleep": tap the button there and wait about a minute.

Put it on your home screen

iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome, Samsung Internet, Edge): tap the three dots, then Add to home screen or Install app.

Then open it from the crimson icon Foul Oracle: it goes straight to the app.

Free, no ads, no account. Swears a lot: choose How rude? in the app's sidebar (Full, Some, Clean or Polite). Astrology has not been found to predict events in controlled studies; this is for fun and reflection. Background image of the app: NASA, ESA, STScI (CC BY 4.0).
